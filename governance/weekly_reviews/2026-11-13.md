## What was learned from new evidence
The source-screening work reinforced three methodological distinctions: (1) workload-trace-based simulated flexibility is not the same as metered site-level grid response; (2) FERC/PJM contract rights are not physical computing flexibility; and (3) modeled dispatch/system cost is not automatically incumbent-ratepayer bill incidence. These are **methodological lessons**, not claims about the student's personal learning.
