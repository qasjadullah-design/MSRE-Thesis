## What the evidence established (not the student's self-reported learning)
- **Engineering feasibility:** the PyPSA-USA data pipeline and zonal HiGHS solve can run for a PJM-relevant extract on Linux; not yet a reliable regional scenario dataset.
- **Model boundary:** the 22-zone ReEDS `PJM` selector approximates actual PJM geography, and external interchange was disabled in smoke tests. Existing fleet and demand allocation require reconciliation.
- **Numerical pitfall:** one-day/one-week cases allowed economic retirement and annualized investment on short time horizons; resulting capacities/prices are artificial, **not thesis findings**.
- **Method distinction:** contract right to interrupt is separate from physical curtailability; zero onsite dispatch is not equivalent to zero installed generator; `epsilon=0` is not legally firm service; interim post-upgrade becomes full NITS.
- **Resource uncertainty:** 17 GB full-hourly-year estimate is a weak linear extrapolation, requiring a third profiling point or a defensible time-reduction/HPC decision.
