### Independent review / qualifications
1. **FCD/NFCD and Interim NITS are FERC co-located/BTMG arrangements, not a generic established non-firm product for arbitrary new loads.** Primary sources: [FERC 193 FERC ¶ 61,217 (2025)](https://www.ferc.gov/sites/default/files/2025-12/E-1%20EL25-49-000.pdf) and [195 FERC ¶ 61,209 (2026)](https://www.ferc.gov/sites/default/files/2026-06/EL25-49-002_0.pdf), including 2026 ¶635 and ¶342. Do not equate G=0 with an eligible co-location arrangement; model it only as an out-of-scope/engineering counterfactual until eligibility is established.
2. The proposal says **ε=0 is the firm case**. This is not legally or analytically automatic: ε is an energy-curtailment tolerance; firmness additionally entails contracted withdrawal rights, adequacy/capacity treatment and service priority. Preserve distinct service-state variables. See FERC 2025/2026 orders above.
3. The claim that **avoided system cost is the largest discount** is at most an *illustrative social-cost bound* and is not an actual tariff or guaranteed ratepayer saving. The discount calculation must specify whether the site's lost-computing disutility is already included, and avoid double subtraction. Consumer cost allocation remains a separate scenario exercise, not actual PJM rate impacts.
4. The proposed interim post-upgrade state should be correctly described as **full NITS**, not an automatic conversion to Firm Contract Demand. FERC 2026 ¶1120 approx., determination on Interim NITS (around printed pp.252-253).

All four remain review comments, not changes to the submitted TH-1 or an approved modeling specification. The repository's official working milestones remain unchanged.
