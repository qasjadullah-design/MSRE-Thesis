# Technical literature evidence matrix — Stage 1 source screening
**Updated:** 12 November 2026
**Shared editable Excel workbook in thesis Google Drive / 01_Literature_PDFs:**
https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1nib8dsSHS-LPxpH8ynS-OQ_CZtc9KStq/edit

This is a **purposive, preliminary** literature matrix with 29 screened sources across six thematic research streams, not a PRISMA-complete systematic review or a set of verified empirical coefficients.

The workbook contains:
1. **START HERE:** theme coverage, evidence classifications and initial objective-level synthesis.
2. **Evidence Matrix:** 36 columns including grid/testbed, mathematical/optimization method, time scale, flexibility and contract representations, data provenance, source-supported result, numeric claim, limitations, usability, URL/DOI, screening status and objective-relevance fields.
3. **Parameter Harvest:** 16 candidate PyPSA inputs with units, sources, missing evidence and next work.
4. **Gap & Decision Map:** 9 provisional gap hypotheses with supporting IDs, caveats, and falsifiable research tests.
5. **Search & Screening Log:** query groups, inclusion/exclusion rules, and records of *incomplete* citation tracing and full-text review.
6. **Source Register** and **Coding Rules**: bibliography and screening-score interpretation.

Important methodological cautions:
- Recorded computing **workload traces** plus simulated power response are NOT equal to metered interruption of full data-center grid imports.
- FERC/PJM **contract rights** are NOT an observed physically achievable data-center curtailment envelope.
- Source outcome coverage is separate from type/strength of evidence.
- Cost from dispatch/capacity expansion is not automatically a ratepayer-bill impact; the latter requires tariff/revenue requirement accounting.
- Any 'gap' here is provisional until complete full-text review and forward/backward citation screening.
- 'NR' means not reported in excerpts screened, NOT that a variable or study was absent.
